# HW-04: the GPIO output level is written before MODER, so the port drives PD1 from "not driven" straight to 1 where v2.05
# first drives it to 0 (0x0801C08A) and then to 1 (0x0801C094).  The wake step "PD1 Z -> 1 with PE9=Z,PD1=Z" needs no
# entry: v2.05 makes it itself at wake (GPIO_Init at 0x0801BD86 with the PD1 output bit already set; whitelist 'power'
# line).  PE9 keeps v2.05's order (src/brd/board.s: Z -> 0 with the port's MODER, PE12 low, then 0 -> 1 at the wake; no
# change at a cold start, where the bootloader has left it high), so it has no entry (apo_stop_wake_short).
HW-04  safety_power  *  pins == PD1 and old == Z and new == 1 and ctx == PE9=1,PD1=Z

# OF-08: calibration-mode entry (MODE at power-on, then REL) does not rewrite the six user-zero records 0x4D0..0x4FC;
# v2.05 writes A5 00 00 00 00 to each (analysis/keys_setup.md 3.1, 5.2).  The scenario's assertions on those bytes
# (lines 12..17) describe v2.05.
OF-08  eeprom        poweron_calmode_erase  offset in 0x4D0..0x4FF
OF-08  eeprom_final  poweron_calmode_erase  offset in 0x4D0..0x4FF
OF-08  assert        poweron_calmode_erase  line in 12..17
# Not writing the six records saves v2.05's six EEPROM programming times (26.6 ms each, 0.842..0.975 s): everything the
# meter does after the REL press of line 10 comes earlier in the port (row write 1.055 s in v2.05, 0.927 s in the baseline:
# 128.6 ms; lcd up to 137.8 ms).
OF-08  tolerance:lcd   poweron_calmode_erase  0.138
OF-08  tolerance:spi   poweron_calmode_erase  0.129
# The clock-chip reads at the end of the power-on window (two full reads) and at the next seconds change come earlier too
# and in another order relative to the 100 ms reads (v2.05 1.006, 1.010, 1.032 s; the baseline 0.869, 0.873, 0.954, 1.007,
# 1.035 s); the 100 ms reads after them are paired again (1.134 / 1.132 s).
OF-08  rtc             poweron_calmode_erase  step == 10 and since in 0.0..0.3

# OF-23: the DIODE 3 V / 15 V selection is not written to EEPROM 0x12 (v2.05: FUN_080159C8 writes it, boot forces 3 V).
